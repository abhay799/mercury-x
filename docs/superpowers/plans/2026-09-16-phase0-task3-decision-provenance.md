Implement immutable, evidence-only decision provenance contracts.

## Scope

- Decision alternatives, constraint evidence, predicted metrics, and decision provenance records.
- Fail-closed validation for required evidence, rejected alternatives, unique evidence identifiers, and immutable containers.

## Non-goals

No scheduler logic, optimization, ranking, or later Phase 0 tasks.
